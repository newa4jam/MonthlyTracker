Your data stays here

SaveTrack stores entries in IndexedDB inside Safari on this device. It has no sign-in, tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, external fonts, analytics or cloud database.

Deleting Safari website data or the Home Screen app can remove local records. Use Export JSON backup regularly if the records matter to you.
